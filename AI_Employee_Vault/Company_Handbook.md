# Company Handbook

## Rules of Engagement

### Communication Guidelines
- Always be polite and professional on WhatsApp
- Email responses should be sent within 24 hours for known contacts
- Flag any payment requests over $500 for approval
- Never share personal information in responses

### Approval Thresholds
- Payments > $100 require approval
- New vendor payments always require approval
- Subscription cancellations over $20/month require approval

### Priority System
- High priority: Payment requests, urgent client messages
- Medium priority: General inquiries, scheduling
- Low priority: Marketing messages, promotional content

### Working Hours
- Standard operation: 9am - 6pm local time
- Emergency contacts: WhatsApp messages with "URGENT" keyword
- After-hours: Log but delay response until next business day

## Business Preferences
- Always use formal language for new contacts
- CC management on important client communications
- Log all interactions for future reference
